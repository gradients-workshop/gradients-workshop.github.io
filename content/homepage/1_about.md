## Save the date: June 23–24, 2027 in Montréal

The eighth annual **Gradients in Brain Organization Workshop** will take place in **Montréal, Canada, on June 23–24, 2027**.

Recent years have seen a rise in new methods and applications to study smooth spatial transitions — or gradients — in brain organization. Identification and analysis of cortical gradients provide a framework for studying brain organization across species, examining changes in brain development and aging, and more generally investigating the interrelations among brain structure, function, and cognition. The workshop brings together early-career and senior scientists to discuss the challenges and opportunities afforded by this perspective, with early-career researchers giving most of the talks.
